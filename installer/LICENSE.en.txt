JARVIS - USAGE NOTICE AND LICENSES

This installer places Jarvis, a Brazilian-Portuguese voice assistant that runs entirely on your computer, in the folder you choose.

[Privacy]
Jarvis does not send your voice, conversations or screen to the internet. The internet is only used to download the AI models (once, over HTTPS, verified by SHA-256). The model server listens on 127.0.0.1 only. The installer does not open ports or change the firewall, does not update drivers, and writes nothing outside the install folder, the shortcuts you choose and (optionally) the "Start with Windows" entry.

[No warranty]
Jarvis is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It can control your computer through allow-listed tools; read the permission explanations in the setup and use the emergency key (Ctrl+Alt+F12) if anything goes wrong.
